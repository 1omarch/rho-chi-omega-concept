Visiting Sorors

Chapter requirements to attend an in-person meeting

Want to invite a soror to visit a Rho Chi Omega chapter meeting? Contact the Membership Chairman at least three (3) business days before the meeting with the information below. Once approved, the visiting soror will receive a personalized invitation, agenda, and additional information — and should bring proof of membership (financial card, Ivy Leaf magazine with their name on it, etc.) for verification.

  • First and last name
  • Last name at time of initiation, if different
  • Initiating chapter or last chapter affiliated with
  • Cell phone number
  • Email address

Contact the Membership Chairman via rhochiomega1908@gmail.com to get started.
