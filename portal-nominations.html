Qualifications

Qualifications of Officers

To be a candidate for any office:

  • A member must be a graduate soror.
  • Must have been financial in Rho Chi Omega for the past 24 consecutive months (October to September) prior to being nominated. To be eligible for the 2026 elections, a soror must be financially active from October 1, 2024, to September 30, 2025, and October 1, 2025, to September 30, 2026.
  • Must have registered and attended a regional conference or Boule or Leadership Conference within the past two (2) years.
  • Must have attended at least six (6) regular meetings between October (prior year) and September (current year).
  • Or, upon approval of the Executive Committee, others may qualify if they meet all other qualifications and have been financially active for twelve (12) consecutive months in the Rho Chi Omega Chapter (October (prior year) to September (current year)).
  • To be a candidate for Chapter Tamiouchos, Anti-Tamiouchos, Pecunious Grammateus, and Anti-Pecunious Grammateus, a candidate shall certify that she has not been sanctioned (1) legally or (2) internally by another organization for misappropriation of funds.

Pan-Hellenic Representatives

  • A member must be a graduate soror.
  • Serve as the liaison between the Rho Chi Omega Chapter and the North Alabama National Pan-Hellenic Council (NPHCNA).
  • Attend North Alabama National Pan-Hellenic Council (NPHCNA) meetings and vote on policies, budgets, event schedules, etc. as a representative of the Rho Chi Omega Chapter.
  • Serve on various North Alabama National Pan-Hellenic Council (NPHCNA) committees as a representative of the Rho Chi Omega Chapter.
  • Promote unity, positive relationships, collective action, and high moral standards with all Divine Nine organizations.
