    Read the full dues policy

    An active member of Rho Chi Omega Chapter shall satisfy all dues and assessments (including the Regional Conference Assessment*) before per capita payment is made on her behalf for a new membership year.

    All dues and assessments are due to the Corporate Office by January 1. To meet this deadline, all chapter dues, chapter assessments, per capita, and Educational Advancement Foundation (EAF) tax are due to the chapter by December 20. If they are not paid by January 1, sorors are considered inactive with both the Corporate Office and the chapter. All monies received after February 1 are subject to a 10% fine.

    All dues and assessments are non-refundable. The per capita for individual sorors is not remitted to the Corporate Office until all assessments, fees, and dues have been collected.

    Exceptions: Sorors reactivating in Rho Chi Omega do not incur the 10% fine on per capita, chapter dues, and chapter assessments. Sorors transferring in Rho Chi Omega incur the 10% fine on per capita after February 1 if not already paid for the year.

    *Sorors who were active in Rho Chi Omega in 2022 and have not paid the SERC Assessment must pay the assessment in order to reactivate or transfer.
