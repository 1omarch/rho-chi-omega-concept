Our Story

Twenty-seven women. One Cultured Pearl legacy.

In the fall of 1987, more than fifty accomplished women united to reclaim and retain sorors across North Alabama. After two years of dedication, twenty-seven of them — revered as the “Cultured Pearls” — were chartered as the Rho Chi Omega Chapter on July 29, 1989.

Today the chapter's signature initiatives, including the Angel Pageant and the Prince to King Mentoring Program, continue to empower youth across Madison and Morgan Counties, alongside more than $100,000 awarded in scholarships.

President's Corner

A message from our 11th president.

Welcome to the website of the Rho Chi Omega Chapter of Alpha Kappa Alpha Sorority, Incorporated®. It is an honor to serve as the 11th president of an exceptional, award-winning chapter dedicated to service, scholarship, and sisterhood.

We are a service organization committed to providing community service throughout Madison and Morgan Counties through scholarships, youth programs, wellness outreach, and community partnerships.

Under the leadership of our International President and CEO, Charletta Wilson Jacks, and her theme, “Championing Service. Empowering Sisterhood. Transforming the World,” Rho Chi Omega remains committed to service, transforming our communities, and addressing critical needs while creating sustainable, long-term impact.

We invite you to explore our website, connect with us on social media, and if you are a member of Alpha Kappa Alpha Sorority, Incorporated® looking for a chapter to call home — we would be honored to have you join us.

Sincerely,
Tiffany Richardson, President

Emerald Community Empowerment Foundation

Powered by service, perpetuated by sisterhood.

The Emerald Community Empowerment Foundation is the official philanthropic affiliate of the Rho Chi Omega Chapter. As a registered IRS 501(c)(3) public charity, the Foundation supports charitable and educational initiatives that uplift, empower, and advocate for communities throughout North Alabama.

Operating independently in governance and finances, the Foundation engages the public to raise and steward funds that support scholarships and community programs aligned with the chapter's mission of service and impact.
