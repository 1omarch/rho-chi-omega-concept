President's Corner

A message from our 11th president.

Welcome to the website of the Rho Chi Omega Chapter of Alpha Kappa Alpha Sorority, Incorporated®. It is an honor to serve as the 11th president of an exceptional, award-winning chapter dedicated to service, scholarship, and sisterhood.

We are a service organization committed to providing community service throughout Madison and Morgan Counties through scholarships, youth programs, wellness outreach, and community partnerships.

Under the leadership of our International President and CEO, Charletta Wilson Jacks, and her theme, “Championing Service. Empowering Sisterhood. We invite you to explore our website, connect with us on social media, and if you are a member of Alpha Kappa Alpha Sorority, Incorporated® looking for a chapter to call home — we would be honored to have you join us.

Sincerely,

Our Story

Our History

The Rho Chi Omega Chapter was established through the shared vision, determination, and sisterly dedication of members of Alpha Kappa Alpha Sorority, Incorporated®, who sought to recruit, reclaim, and retain inactive sorors throughout North Alabama. In the fall of 1987, more than fifty accomplished and diverse women united with purpose to form a new Graduate Chapter serving Madison and Morgan Counties, along with surrounding communities.

After years of steadfast commitment and perseverance, twenty-seven women—revered as the “Cultured Pearls”—brought this vision to fruition. On July 29, 1989, they were proudly chartered as the Rho Chi Omega Chapter of Alpha Kappa Alpha Sorority, Incorporated®, marking the beginning of a legacy rooted in service, excellence, and sisterhood.

Our Impact

Since its inception, Rho Chi Omega has faithfully upheld the Sorority’s enduring mission of Service to All Mankind. The chapter remains deeply committed to supporting a wide range of local and national organizations, creating meaningful and lasting impact throughout the community.

Today, with a membership of more than 184 active sorors, the chapter continues to thrive in both scope and service. Signature initiatives such as the Angel Pageant and the Prince to King Mentoring Program are cherished community programs that empower, inspire, and uplift youth across the region. In addition, Rho Chi Omega proudly champions educational advancement, having awarded more than $100,000 in scholarships to deserving college-bound students—an investment in future leaders and lifelong learners.

Our Commitment

Rho Chi Omega remains steadfast in its dedication to the International Program Theme: “Championing Service. Empowering Sisterhood. Transforming the World.” Guided by the founding principles of Alpha Kappa Alpha Sorority, Incorporated®—sisterhood, service, leadership, and community uplift—the chapter continues to make a powerful difference. United in purpose and passion, we proudly honor our rich legacy while boldly shaping its future through innovation, global engagement, and transformational service!

Emerald Community Empowerment Foundation

Powered by service, perpetuated by sisterhood.

The Emerald Community Empowerment Foundation is the official philanthropic affiliate of the Rho Chi Omega Chapter. As a registered IRS 501(c)(3) public charity, the Foundation supports charitable and educational initiatives that uplift, empower, and advocate for communities throughout North Alabama.

Operating independently in governance and finances, the Foundation engages the public to raise and steward funds that support scholarships and community programs aligned with the chapter's mission of service and impact.

In Loving Memory

A Tribute to AKA Ivies Beyond the Wall

Our Ivies Beyond the Wall,
Our Dear Sisters of A-K-A
Have been the truest of all
And have shown us a better way.

Their endless joys now begun,
They’ve heard the Master’s “Welcome Voice,”
Let’s praise them with words, “Well done,”
And in their honor let’s rejoice.

Our Sorors, they are not dead,
Because they walk with us no more;
They’re on their way home instead,
And leaving us, have gone before.

As the evening shadows come,
We bid our Sisters their farewells;
In life we all must succumb,
Being Earth’s dust and empty shells.

We ask God’s mercy and prayers,
Meditating from day to day;
Help us, Lord, to climb our stairs,
Guide us, keep us along the way.

Soror Carrie M. Shedd
(Source: An AKA’s Poetic Leisure, 1981)
