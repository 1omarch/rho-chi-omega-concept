How It Works

Three simple steps.

Step 1

Engage

Participate in eligible Rho Chi Omega activities including meetings, service projects, programs, surveys, committees, workshops, and chapter initiatives.

Step 2

Earn

Eligible participation earns RCO Rewards Points that are added to your personal rewards account.

Step 3

Enjoy

Redeem your points for available rewards or keep stacking your balance toward something bigger.

The more you engage, the more you earn—and YOU decide when you enjoy the reward.
